Delete a MediTool account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open MediTool and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, scroll to Your account, and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type the word shown on the screen and tap Delete everything.

The account is deleted at once.

Without the app

Send an email to sgmeditool@gmail.com from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my MediTool account". The account is deleted within 7 days, and you get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything: the account (email and password), every person in it, and all their blood and urine results, readings, medicines, reminders, notes and settings. Nothing is kept. Copies in the database's automatic backups disappear when those backups expire, within 30 days.
