MediTool privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

MediTool is an app for keeping a family's health records: blood and urine test results, blood pressure, glucose, oxygen and weight readings, and medicines with reminders. This page says what MediTool keeps, where, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What MediTool keeps

  • Your account: your email address and your password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • The people you add: name, and if you fill them in, date of birth, sex, height, blood type and allergies.
  • Health records you add: readings (blood pressure, pulse, glucose, oxygen, weight), blood and urine test results, medicines and their reminder times, and notes.
  • Your settings: language, units, date and time format, text size.

MediTool does not use your location, contacts, or advertising IDs. It has no ads and no tracking or analytics tools.

Where it is kept

Your records are stored on Supabase, a database service, so they are the same on every phone you sign in on. Each account can only read its own records; this is enforced by the database itself. Data is encrypted while it travels between the app and the server.

On your phone, MediTool keeps your sign-in and your settings, so the app can open without the internet for a moment. The app locks itself after a few minutes untouched, when it is closed, and after a minute in another app.

Photos and PDF files

When you take a photo of a machine, a medicine box or a lab report, or choose a PDF, the file is sent to MediTool's server and from there to Google's Gemini AI service, which reads the numbers and names in it. MediTool saves only the results you approve on the screen, not the photo or the file. Google processes the file under its own terms for the Gemini API.

Sending results to a doctor

When you use "Send to a doctor", the PDF is made on your phone and goes only where you choose in your phone's Share menu (for example WhatsApp or email). MediTool does not send it anywhere else and does not keep a copy on the server.

Who can see your records

Only you, signed in to your account. MediTool does not sell, rent or share your records with anyone, and does not use them for advertising. The services above (Supabase for storage, Google for reading photos) handle the data only to provide those functions.

Medicine reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. They are not sent through any server.

Deleting your account and records

In the app: Settings › Delete my account. This erases the account and every person, result, reading, medicine, note and setting in it, at once. It cannot be undone.

Without the app: see how to delete a MediTool account.

Children

An account is opened by an adult. An adult may keep records for a child or another family member they look after.

Not medical advice

MediTool keeps records and shows them as lists and graphs. It does not diagnose and does not give medical advice. Ask a doctor about your results.
